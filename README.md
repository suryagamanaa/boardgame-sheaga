# SHE-aga: Journey to Financial Resilience

A financial simulation game for women. It teaches financial risk through an interactive experience, so that risk feels real before it happens.

SHE-aga is one of three tools in **SHE-UP! (Sinergi Hidup Ekonomi Perempuan)**, a financial literacy program by team TRIHAS (IPB University), supported by Astra through the ACTION! program.

Live Demo: https://boardgame-sheaga.vercel.app/

## Why a game

Many people stop at "understanding" and "feeling" a financial risk, but never reach "doing" something about it. Gamification and simulation turn risk into an emotional experience, and playing together makes protective action feel safe and normal.

## What it is

- A physical board game and an online version.
- Players move through life events that affect their income, spending, and protection.
- Example tiles: **Pemasukan Tambahan**, **Motor Rusak**, **Challenge YUK!**, **Perlindungan!**, and **Bankrupt**.
- QR codes on the board lead to the SHE-aga Microsite, where learning continues.

## Learning goals

- **Know:** understand common financial risks and why an emergency fund matters.
- **Protect:** see how insurance and protection reduce the impact of unexpected events.
- **Sustain:** build the habit of recording and planning, with support from a community.
